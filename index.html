Three ways to play

Every player finds their own footing here — dig in for the long haul, or drop into something quick.

⛏

Survival

The classic grind. Build your base, gear up, raid the Nether, and hold your ground against everything the world throws at you — with a community that's actually online.

🟫

OneBlock

Start on a single floating block above the void. Break it, get resources, and watch your island grow phase by phase. Simple rule, surprisingly hard to put down.

🎮

Minigames

Quick rounds when you don't have time for a full session — PvP arenas, parkour runs, and community events that rotate to keep things fresh.
